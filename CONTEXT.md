# Borulanta

Borulanta, a Wednesday football team: who played, what each match cost them, what they have paid, and how the team does in its league.

## Language

### People

**Player**:
Anyone named in the records of a match, whether or not they were ever charged.
_Avoid_: member

**Guest**:
A player off the roster who is named only as a scorer or man of the match; never part of a squad and never charged.
_Avoid_: using it for a rostered player who is not core

**Roster**:
The players who are charged under a season's fee rules, fixed per season.
_Avoid_: squad, members

**Core player**:
A rostered player who carries the shortfall of a small squad under that season's fee rules, whether or not they played.
_Avoid_: regular, legacy core; a rostered player who is not core has no special name

**Squad**:
The rostered players present at one match; a guest who played is not part of it.
_Avoid_: lineup, team, attendance

### Matches

**Match**:
One league game Borulanta played, with its score, whether or not the sync recorded it.
_Avoid_: game, result

**Fixture**:
A league match not yet played, as listed on the league page.
_Avoid_: upcoming match

**Opponent**:
The other team in a match.

**Covered match**:
A match recorded by the sync from the league page, so its goals and man of the match are complete.

**Attribution**:
The player a Borulanta goal is counted for, which can differ from the league page.
_Avoid_: credit

**Unattributed goal**:
A Borulanta goal counted for no player: an own goal, or one scored by someone with no record here.
_Avoid_: uncredited goal

### Money

**Season**:
A stretch of matches priced by one set of fee rules; it starts when the rules change, not on a calendar date.
_Avoid_: year

**Fee rules**:
The formula that turns one match's squad and roster into charges.
_Avoid_: pricing, fee policy

**Match fee**:
The pitch cost of one match, which the charges for that match add up to.
_Avoid_: pitch fee

**Charge**:
What one rostered player owes for one match, including a zero.
_Avoid_: fee, cost

**Payment**:
Money a player handed over, on a date, against their charges.

**Balance**:
A player's charges less their payments, over all time; positive means they owe, negative means they are **in credit**.
_Avoid_: debt

**Ledger**:
A closed season's charges, frozen so they no longer depend on the fee rules.
_Avoid_: archive
